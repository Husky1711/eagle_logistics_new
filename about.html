About Us

Eagle Logistics is one of India’s leading courier and logistics providers, serving individuals, industries, commercial establishments and educational institutions. We offer door-to-door pickup and delivery of documents, books, stationery, clothing, footwear, non-perishable food, electronics and more, across India and to major destinations worldwide including the USA, UK, European Union, Middle East and Far East, at competitive rates.

01

Doorstep or local pickup

We can collect your parcel from your doorstep or a local pickup point and deliver it through our reliable, tracked logistics network, saving you time and money.

02

Safety you can trust

We use stringent safety measures to prevent loss, pilferage or damage. We are among the few courier companies that also provide international door-to-door cargo.

03

Tracked every step

Our customer service team uses online tracking and communication tools for round-the-clock visibility and prompt delivery. Shipments are booked through recognised express partners.

Vision

To be recognised as the largest and most trusted courier company in the country, with nationwide reach; to remain an economical, cost-effective service; and to set new benchmarks in productivity, customer satisfaction and employee satisfaction.

Mission

Be the link between you and your loved ones. Serve you with dedicated, passionate staff. Give you full transparency throughout delivery. Deliver the best possible customer service experience.

Values
