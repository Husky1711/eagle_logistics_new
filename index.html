Why ship with Eagle

Established in 2010, Eagle Logistics offers competitive rates for international parcels from India to the USA, UK, European Union, Middle East and Far East. Arrange doorstep collection or drop off at a local pickup point, then track your shipment through our reliable partner network. We specialise in local, national and international courier with careful handling and international door-to-door cargo.
