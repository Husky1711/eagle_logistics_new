Medicines

Ensuring the right equipment and supplies are in the right hands in the right place at the right time is what drives our medical logistics and supply chain management services. We combine subject matter experts (SMEs) with logistics information systems specialists to help our customers in the planning, implementation, including warehousing, freight boarding, and inventory management and accountability.

Supply chain visibility is considered the major challenge facing the industry. Efficiency gains, packaging and labelling, and careful handling matter when shipping medicines. From point of origin to destination, Eagle Logistics provides easy storage, ordering, fulfillment, shipping, continuous tracking and monitoring, documentation assistance and 24/7 customer support for your cold chain logistics needs.
